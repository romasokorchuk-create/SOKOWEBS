FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need to bring the car anywhere?

No. We come to you — home, office, wherever the car is parked, anywhere in the Sacramento area.

What are your hours?

8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, seven days a week. Weekends at the same price.

How long will it take?

Interior and exterior: 1–1.5 hours. A full detail: 2–2.5 hours. Stain removal and paint correction are quoted per car, 2 hours held. Plus 30 minutes for travel and setup.

Why does my slot show extra time?

We build the drive and setup into the appointment instead of hiding it. The time you pick is the time we start.

Is the price fixed?

The listed price is the standard range. Heavy pet hair or a neglected interior takes longer. Any change, we tell you before we start — not after.

How do I book?

Use the form above, or call or text (916) 595-8574. We confirm every request by text.
